# M8.1 comparison placement card

## Decision

Comparison management uses the historical photo-placing result-card image pair. Comparison-result detail keeps its existing presentation; it is not part of this correction.

## UI contract

- `WITH_CONTEST` renders the award and personal photo as equal 4:3 previews.
- The pair is exactly `grid grid-cols-2`: no gap, label, arrow, frame, per-image padding, or breakpoint that stacks the pair.
- The management screen owns the title, visibility state, control, pending state, and error below the image pair.
- `PHOTO_ONLY` renders one personal-photo preview.

## Non-goals

Visibility mutation, its pending/error feedback, comparison APIs, and personal album metadata boundaries are unchanged.
